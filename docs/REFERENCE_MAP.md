# Reference Map

The detailed source catalog is `catalogs/references.json`. It stores source URL, category, purpose, and verification status. This is provenance metadata, not a claim that every page has been fully audited line-by-line.

## Categories
1. Design Direction — visual identity, layout, product specificity and anti-slop.
2. Review & Audit — interface, writing, robustness and repair workflows.
3. Typography — modular scale, audit and web rendering.
4. 3D & Shaders — Three.js, scroll worlds and shader authoring.
5. Design Systems — design tokens, component systems and theme architecture.
6. Motion & Animation — purposeful motion, gesture, physics and performance.
7. Color & Theming — palette generation, semantic color, OKLCH and contrast.

Verification status meanings:
- `verified`: source content was accessed and used in the prior research session.
- `partial`: the source was located or partially reviewed, but full source-level verification was incomplete.
- `unverified`: source could not be accessed. Do not attribute detailed claims to it until rechecked.
